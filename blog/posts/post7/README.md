## Sources and definitions

All four series originate with the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and are distributed through FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Each is monthly, seasonally adjusted, and an index with 1982–1984=100:

| Display label | FRED ID | Official description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All items | [CPIAUCSL](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL) | CPI for all urban consumers, all items, U.S. city average |
| Groceries | [CUSR0000SAF11](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CUSR0000SAF11) | CPI for all urban consumers, food at home, U.S. city average |
| Rent | [CUSR0000SEHA](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CUSR0000SEHA) | CPI for all urban consumers, rent of primary residence, U.S. city average |
| Gasoline | [CUSR0000SETB01](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CUSR0000SETB01) | CPI for all urban consumers, gasoline (all types), U.S. city average |

Download route used for the supplied file:
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=CPIAUCSL,CUSR0000SAF11,CUSR0000SEHA,CUSR0000SETB01&cosd=2019-01-01

The supplied CSV contains 897 rows because the component series include earlier histories. The analysis keeps the 93 calendar months from January 2019 through September 2026. The chart begins in January 2020. The common endpoint is September 2026; both default comparison months have all four observations.

October 2025 is missing for all items, groceries, and rent; gasoline is observed. No missing values are fabricated, interpolated, or forward-filled. Annual inflation uses the same calendar month one year earlier, not the twelfth previously observed row. A rate is missing if either required endpoint is missing. An unavailable common month is excluded from the baseline selector, without deleting the month from the chart's timeline.

Price change from baseline month `b` to month `t` is `100 * (P[t] / P[b] - 1)`.
Annual inflation is `100 * (P[t] / P[t-12 calendar months] - 1)`.
The calculation uses the original indexes and rounds only displayed results to one decimal place. These annual changes are calculated from seasonally adjusted series and may differ from BLS's unadjusted twelve-month headline changes. Price indexes do not establish wage growth, personal affordability, or household welfare.

Documentation: https://plotly.com/javascript/getting-started/

## Interactive controls

- **Price change:** choose a baseline month to recompute all displayed percentage changes. January 2020 and June 2022 shortcuts support the two comparisons discussed in the article.
- **Annual inflation:** compare each month with the same month one year earlier. The price-baseline selector is disabled because it has no role in this measure.
- **Categories:** show or hide series; at least one stays selected. Endpoint cards update with the chosen measure and baseline.
- **Hover and zoom:** inspect monthly values and isolate periods. Zoom affects the display only; it does not change the selected comparison month.

The static table in the article supplies the main numerical comparisons for readers who cannot use the interactive figure. Missing observations remain visible as gaps in the chart.
