Blog 9 data and method sources

1. Uploaded real market data: blog9_market_data.zip, supplied by the author after
   running blog9_download_data.ipynb in Google Colab. Provider: Yahoo Finance,
   obtained via yfinance 0.2.66 with auto_adjust=False, repair=False, keepna=True.
   https://finance.yahoo.com/
   https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.download.html
   Downloaded 2026-11-09 03:15 UTC (2026-11-08 22:15 America/New_York).
   Raw data span 2022-01-03 through 2026-11-06: 1,217 dates x 96 symbols.
   prices.csv and download_quality.csv preserve the uploaded bytes exactly.
   manifest.json preserves the original coverage, package versions and checksums.
   The universe is the instructor's 95 named equities plus SPY, not the historical
   membership of a comprehensive or survivorship-free stock index.

2. Original classroom program: UPenn_XGBoostStockPrediction copy.R, provided in
   the author's course materials. Preserved verbatim as code/original_class_model.R.
   See code/CLASS_MODEL_AUDIT.txt for the Python-port mapping and common repairs.

3. Shared class sheet, Stock Portfolio Results:
   https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Busi9v-ibPt8WwThnLs94uK7Tz-XDa5EOc6cLDt3nvQ/edit
   Accessed 2026-11-08 America/New_York. Only aggregated pair counts are retained
   in classroom_context.json; individual reported balances are not republished.
   Nine rows have both balances: the second is larger in six, smaller in three.
   One unpaired row is excluded. No dates, initial balances, costs or trading
   rules are given, so the sheet is contextual evidence, not model validation.

4. XGBoost parameters and regularization (official documentation):
   https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/parameter.html
   https://parsnip.tidymodels.org/reference/details_boost_tree_xgboost.html
   The latter documents min_n -> min_child_weight and the other R parameter maps.

All reported empirical returns, predictions and figures are computed from the
uploaded real data. The block bootstrap only resamples observed forecast losses;
it does not generate or substitute stock prices. No generated market data are used.
